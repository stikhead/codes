4. transfer learning

to figure out exactly what those 768 floats should be for every word,
and to perfectly tune the self-attention matrices, we need to feed the AI millions
of wikipedia articles and run thousands of gpus 24/7 for months.

to avoid this, there are several organizations that provides "pre-trained" models that natively understands the grammar, 
embeddings, and attention mechanics of 100 of languages, 
they then exported all those calculated floats and matrix weights into a .bin file

"fine-tuning" means downloading google's completed .bin file,
freezing 99% of architecture so it cant be altered, and only spending ~30 minutes updating the 
very last output layer to a specific task. it is the machine learning equivalent of importing a 
highly optimized c++ binary library instead of wiriting our own operating system from scratch
