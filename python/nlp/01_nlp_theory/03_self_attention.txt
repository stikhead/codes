3. Self Attention

word embeddings are static, the dictionary will always assign the exact same 768 floats
to word "bank". but what if the sentence is "the bank of the river" vs "the bank was robbed"?
if the floats are the same, the ai is blind to the Context

the Attention algorithm is a solution to this. When we pass a sentence into bert,
the ai doesnt read left to right. it loads all the words into a massive matrix at once. 
it then forces every single word to mathematically "look" at every other word in the sentence

when the word "bank" looks around and sees "river", 
the attention mechanism performs a matrix multiplication that pulls the bank's floats slightly closer
to the coordinates for "water".
if it sees "robbed", it pulls the floats closer to "money". 
The AI dynamically updates the meaning of every word on the fly based on the surrounding data.
this is the exact breakthrough that makes LLMs possible.
